# Vancouver City Guide

Vancouver is a coastal city in British Columbia known for mountains, ocean views, parks, multicultural food, and outdoor recreation.

## Stanley Park

Stanley Park is a large urban park near downtown Vancouver.

The park contains walking paths, cycling routes, forested areas, beaches, viewpoints, and the seawall.

Cycling or walking a portion of the seawall is a popular outdoor activity. Visitors should allow several hours if they plan to explore multiple areas of the park.

## Granville Island

Granville Island contains a public market, food vendors, artisan shops, galleries, and performance spaces.

It can be reached by road, public transportation, or small passenger ferries.

The public market is useful for casual meals and local food products.

## Downtown and Waterfront

Canada Place and the Coal Harbour waterfront provide walking areas and views of the harbour and mountains.

Gastown is known for historic buildings, restaurants, shops, and its steam-clock area.

Visitors can combine Gastown, Canada Place, and Coal Harbour because they are located relatively close to one another.

## Nature and Day Trips

Grouse Mountain offers mountain views and seasonal activities.

Capilano Suspension Bridge Park is located in North Vancouver and requires additional transportation time from downtown.

English Bay is a waterfront area with beaches, walking paths, and sunset views.

## Transportation

Vancouver's public transportation system includes buses, SkyTrain, and SeaBus services.

SkyTrain connects downtown with several surrounding areas and the airport.

SeaBus connects downtown Vancouver with the North Shore.

Walking and cycling are practical in many central areas, especially along the waterfront and around Stanley Park.

## Sample Planning Advice

Stanley Park should receive at least half a day when cycling or walking the seawall.

Granville Island can be combined with nearby waterfront activities.

North Shore attractions require extra transportation time and should not be added to an already crowded downtown schedule.
